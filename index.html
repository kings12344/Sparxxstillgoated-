I'm $parxx$tillgoated. I make music from the parts of life people usually keep to themselves.

Raised between Mahwalala, Mbabane and Ezulwini, my sound moves between rap, melody and the things that stay on your mind after the night ends. The confidence is there. So is the heartbreak. Neither one is an act.
